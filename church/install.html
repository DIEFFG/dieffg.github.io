Put the Church App
on your phone or computer

Free. No app store needed. Takes about one minute.

The church app addresshttps://dieffg.github.io/church/

Open the church app

iPhone or iPad

Use the blue compass app called Safari.

  1. Open Safari and type the church app address above. (Do not open it inside Facebook or WhatsApp.)
  2. Tap the Share button (a square with an arrow pointing up). It is at the bottom of the screen, or at the top on an iPad.
  3. Scroll down the list and tap Add to Home Screen .
  4. Tap Add (top right). The church icon now sits on your Home Screen.
  5. From now on, open the church app from that icon.
For alerts on iPhone: they only work when you open the app from the Home Screen icon (needs iOS 16.4 or newer). Then tap the bell, tap Turn on alerts, and tap Allow.

Android phone or tablet

Use the round red-yellow-green-blue app called Chrome.

  1. Open Chrome and type the church app address above.
  2. Tap the three dots at the top right.
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  4. Tap Install. The church icon appears with your other apps.
For alerts: open the app, tap the bell, tap Turn on alerts, then tap Allow.

Windows computer

Easiest way: let the installer file do it for you.

  1. Find the file called Install-Church (the church office will give it to you).
  2. Double-click it. If Windows asks, choose Run anyway (click More info first).
  3. A black window says what it is doing. When it says Done, press any key.
  4. Look on your Desktop: there is now a Christian Sanctuary icon. Double-click it any time.
No installer file? Open the address in Edge or Chrome, then click the small install picture at the right end of the address bar and click Install.

Mac computer

Pick the way that is easiest for you.

  1. Open the address in Safari.
  2. In the top menu, click File, then Add to Dock.
  3. Click Add. The church icon is now in your Dock.
Or use the installer file: find Install-Church.command, right-click it, choose Open, then click Open again. It puts Christian Sanctuary in your Applications.

Need help?

  1. Ask the church office, or ask a friend to sit with you for one minute.
  2. Not signed in yet? Tap Member sign in, type your email, and we email you a 6-digit code. No password.
  3. The app keeps the last pages you looked at, so it still opens when your signal is weak.
